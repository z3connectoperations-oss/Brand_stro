Creative Brief & Client Mandate

Primary Objective

Create a modern, premium, trustworthy visual identity for ABC Foods catering to urban consumers seeking healthy, unadulterated artisanal staple foods.

Target Demographic

Young urban professionals and health-conscious families (ages 22–40) purchasing via tier-1 supermarkets and direct-to-consumer grocery apps.

Brand Personality & Tonal Attributes
Modern Trustworthy Premium FMCG Approachable Luxury Warm Organic Palette
Client Preferences & Hard Guardrails Strict Compliance
  • Favor minimalist geometric typography with clean optical balance over ornate scripts.
  • Incorporate subtle warm ochre/saffron tones; avoid neon greens or generic clip-art wheat leaves.
  • Must scale crisply from 16px digital app icons to 12-foot warehouse delivery fleet liveries.
