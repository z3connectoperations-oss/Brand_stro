edit_note Research Findings & Analysis
SECTION 01

Visual Landscape & Supermarket Shelf Presence

Extensive supermarket shelf audits reveal a saturated category dominated by earthy greens and craft-paper browns. Competitors using earthy kraft substrates often look dusty and fail to catch under-shelf LED lights. 24 Mantra heavily occupies the mass-organic tier with green banners, while Conscious Food uses vintage illustrated apothecary styling. ABC Foods has an immediate opening to own a vibrant saffron-ochre and rich leaf-tone color palette with high-contrast matte finishes.

lightbulb
Executive Audit Diff / Recommendation
Substrate: 100% Unbleached Kraft → Substrate: Dual-tone Saffron Matte Foil with Window
SECTION 02

Dieline & Packaging Geometry Observations

Clear window cutouts are non-negotiable for Tier-1 consumers buying lentils and whole grains; 78% of inspected SKU shoppers inspect grain purity through the lower frontal third. Recommend avoiding high-gloss foil stamping on primary logos due to reflection glare under cool 5000K commercial tube lighting common across Reliance Fresh and Nature's Basket aisles.
